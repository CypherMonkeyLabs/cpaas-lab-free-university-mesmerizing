Contact center words that stay general

  • The IVR article says an IVR gathers information and routes calls, and that an ACD and an automated attendant are separate systems it is often combined with.
  • A contact center, in ordinary speech, is broader than one vendor's queue. This lab does not invent a handle-time formula.
  • Twilio's voice navigation names an inbound call center use case, Queue, and TaskRouter on the docs home.
  • TaskRouter's field list was not opened.
  • Bandwidth's home page names a Salesforce Contact Center integration. The field map was not opened.
